How It Works

Two services. No confusion
about what happens next.

Here is exactly what to expect, from first contact to the finished result.

Google Review Card
From order to first review
1
We add your business to our catalogue

We collect your business name and Google review link so your card is programmed correctly the first time. No setup on your end.

2
You get a tap card programmed to your review page

Your card arrives ready to use. No app, no login, no software. It works the moment you hand it over.

3
Hand it to customers after great service

One tap on a customer's phone opens your Google review page directly. No searching, no typing your business name.

4
Watch your reviews grow

We track your review count over time and check in periodically. If you want updates or additional cards, we are available.

Website Creation
From intake to live site
1
You fill out our intake form

We send you a personalized link. You fill in your business details, brand preferences, services, and hours at your own pace. Progress saves automatically.

2
We build your site

Using your intake information, we build a professional, mobile-friendly website tailored to your business. Typically done within 2 to 5 business days.

3
You review and approve

We send you a preview link. You review it, we make adjustments, and once you are happy we move to launch.

4
We handle launch and setup

We connect your domain, set up HTTPS, and make sure everything is working correctly before handing you the keys.

5
Optional ongoing maintenance

Need to update hours, swap photos, or add a new service page? We offer flexible maintenance plans so your site stays current without the hassle.
